By hand

This browser cannot write to the card from a page. Chrome and Edge can, and there the copy is one button and it checks every part. Here the files are plain downloads, so take the card out of the reader and put it in the computer, in its slot or in a card reader.

    Make a folder called wikipedia at the root of the card. Put dict.zst, titles.idx and blocks.dir in it, the parts in a folder called shards inside it, and manifest.json last: the reader treats the pack as present the moment it sees the manifest, so it goes in after everything it names. Then put the card back in the reader and open Wikipedia.

    The articles are Wikipedia's, under CC BY-SA 4.0, packed for a screen that holds still. Nothing about you leaves this tab; the page asks the pack's host for the files and writes them to the card you chose, and touches nothing else on it.
