Live · on somebody's fridge

Leave a note on their reader.

Draw, write or send a picture here, and a reader asleep on a fridge in another country shows it. It wakes on its own schedule to look, so something sent tonight is there in the morning.

Connect to a reader

On the reader: Apps › Wallpapers › Your phone. It shows six digits. Somebody can read them to you over the telephone; you do not need to be there.

The code lasts ten minutes.

Pen

Anyone with this page connected to that reader can put a picture on it. Access is taken away on the reader itself, which is the point: when the person sending is in another country, the device is the one thing anybody can physically reach. On it, Apps › Wallpapers › Your phone.
