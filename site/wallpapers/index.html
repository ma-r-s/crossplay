Getting it onto the reader

  1. Download the wallpaper above. It is a small .bmp sized to the screen.

  2. On the reader, open File Transfer from the home screen and follow it to the address it shows in your browser. (Or plug the reader in over USB.)

  3. Copy the .bmp into the wallpapers folder on the card. The reader makes that folder the first time you open the Wallpapers app.

  4. Open Apps › Wallpapers on the reader, tap your wallpaper, and it becomes the sleep screen.
