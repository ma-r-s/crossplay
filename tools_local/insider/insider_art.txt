# Cut at 96px, not the shelf's 32: these are the whole content of the reveal
# card, held up to one person's face and then passed on. A role has to be
# readable in the second and a half somebody looks at it, which is why the card
# carries a silhouette at all rather than just the word MASTER.
#
# The three have to be unmistakable from each other at a glance, so they are
# built from three different shapes rather than three variations on an eye:
# a mask has a horizontal double lobe, the scroll is a vertical rectangle with
# a curl, and the closed eye is a single lidded arc.
#
# roleInsider: venetian-mask. It is the only icon on this device that means
#   "pretending", which is the entire job, and it beats a plain `eye` -- the
#   Insider's secret is not that they can see, it is that they are hiding it.
# roleMaster: scroll-text. They hold the word and read from it. Rejected:
#   `crown` (chess owns it on the shelf), `gavel` (says courtroom, and the
#   courtroom part of this game is the vote, which the Master sits out),
#   `user-round` (a person, when the other two are states of knowing).
# roleCitizen: eye-closed. The negation of seeing, drawn as one lidded arc.
#   `eye-off` was rejected here even though it is the fork's existing mark for
#   an unreadable link: at 96px its slash is the loudest thing on the card, and
#   a Citizen is not an error.
